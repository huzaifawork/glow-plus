How it works

The same psychology as a punch card on your fridge — just one that never gets lost, and updates itself.
01 — Visit

Visit

Get the style, service, or treatment you always get — at any salon on Glow+. Each one builds its own menu, so what you see is exactly what they offer.

02 — Earn

Earn

Your stylist logs the visit and your points land instantly. Every style is worth a set number of points, no receipt-scanning required.

03 — Redeem

Redeem

Watch your punch card fill in real time. When you hit the salon's threshold, your discount or free service unlocks automatically.
