How it is protected

MeasureWhat it means
PasswordsHashed with bcrypt (cost factor 12). They cannot be read or recovered by anyone, including us — a forgotten password can only be reset, never retrieved.
Phone numbersEncrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM before being written to the database. Anyone reading the raw database sees ciphertext, not a number.
In transitAll traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. The service sends HSTS, so browsers refuse to connect insecurely.
Access controlEvery request is authenticated with a short-lived token (15 minutes). A salon can only ever see its own customers' visits, never another salon's.
DatabaseNot reachable from the public internet. Encrypted at rest by the hosting provider, in addition to the field-level encryption above.

How long we keep it

Your account and its visit history are kept for as long as your account exists, because that history is what your rewards are calculated from. Unused points expire after 365 days of inactivity at a salon. Password-reset links expire within an hour of being issued, and sign-in sessions expire far sooner. When you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal information as described below.

Your rights

  • Correct anything inaccurate.
  • Delete your account and personal information. Records a salon must keep for its own accounting may be retained in anonymised form — with your name, email and phone number removed — so that deleting your account cannot corrupt a salon's own history.
