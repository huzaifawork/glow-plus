Points and rewards

Rewards are offered by each salon, not by Glow+. Each salon decides its own services, how many points a visit is worth, and what a reward is. We provide the software that keeps count.

  • Points have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money, and cannot be transferred between people or between salons.
  • Points expire after 365 days without a visit to that salon.
  • A reward can be redeemed once per milestone reached. Redeeming is recorded so it cannot be claimed twice.
  • If a salon leaves Glow+, points earned there can no longer be redeemed. We cannot compensate you for them, and we encourage salons to honour outstanding rewards before leaving.
